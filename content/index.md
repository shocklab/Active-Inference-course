Active inference brings together inference, learning and action. This course
builds the mathematics behind those connections, with intermediate steps and
worked examples for readers who know calculus, linear algebra and probability.
No background in neuroscience or machine learning is assumed.

The lessons pair derivations with interactive examples. Change a prior, compare
two inference methods, or vary the cost of a decision while holding the evidence
fixed. Companion Python notebooks let you inspect the calculations and extend
them.

Weeks 1 and 2 are available. The remaining ten weeks are planned, moving from
variational inference to continuous dynamics and then discrete decision models.
The course is free to read and its source is on GitHub.
